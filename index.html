Engineering Detail

The Wall System

Every FUSIONest wall is a precision-engineered assembly of 7 distinct layers — from a robust MS structural frame to a smooth interior finish.

01
🎨
Interior Finishing
Smooth, painted interior wall surface — ready for your design vision.
Innermost Layer
02
🪵
Internal Cement Fibre Board
Rigid, durable 12mm board — structural substrate for internal finish.
Cement Fibre Board
03
🟡
Vapour Barrier Membrane
Yellow moisture-control membrane — blocks condensation from inside.
Moisture Protection
04
🔥
GI Studs + Rockwool Insulation
C-channel GI stud frame with Rockwool packed in every cavity — thermal & acoustic core.
Core Insulated Frame
05
🟡
Vapour Barrier Membrane
Exterior-side moisture membrane — prevents rain-driven damp ingress.
Weather Barrier
06
🪵
External Cement Fibre Board
Rigid external-face board — base for all exterior cladding and finishing.
Cement Fibre Board
07
🏠
External Finishing
Weather-resistant exterior texture coat — protects and beautifies.
Outermost Layer
🏗️
MS Structural Frame — The Backbone Behind the entire wall assembly sits the primary MS (Mild Steel) structural frame — columns, beams and joists engineered to carry all loads. Every wall panel, every layer is built upon this precision steel skeleton.

Why Choose Prefab?

See how the FUSIONest approach compares to traditional construction methods.

⚡ FUSIONest Prefab Approach
Controlled, precision-engineered construction process
Significantly faster installation timeline
Higher material quality control and consistency
Reduced on-site waste and site disruption
Predictable timelines and cost management
Engineering-driven design with structural accuracy
Integrated MEP coordination within the wall system
Modern aesthetic, customizable to any style
VS
Traditional Site-Heavy Construction
Highly variable quality dependent on labour and conditions
Longer construction timelines with weather dependencies
Less consistent material usage and handling
Higher site waste and disruption to surroundings
Delays common due to coordination issues
Structural performance harder to verify
MEP coordination often resolved on-site
Finish quality can vary significantly
